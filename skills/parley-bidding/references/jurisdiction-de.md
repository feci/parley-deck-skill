# Germany jurisdiction profile

This reference is a cited workflow aid, not legal advice and not a universal default. The buyer's current documents and binding communications control.

## Record, do not infer

- procedure type and applicable procurement regime stated by the buyer;
- lots, variants, consortium, subcontractor, and suitability rules;
- required form, language, electronic submission method, and signature regime;
- deadlines with Europe/Berlin offset and portal-displayed time;
- bidder/declarant authority basis;
- exclusion, self-declaration, register, certificate, and evidence requirements;
- negotiation status and first-offer risk;
- offer validity/binding period;
- tax/VAT treatment as a named finance decision; and
- contract hierarchy, deviations, and required forms.

Platform technical acceptance is not proof of procedural or legal validity. Escalate authority, signature, tax, exclusion, and material contract questions to the named human or counsel.

## Useful official discovery context

- Datenservice Öffentlicher Einkauf / `oeffentlichevergabe.de`: public German notice discovery/open data.
- TED: EU notice publication.
- `service.bund.de`: publication surface, not automatically the submission system.

Recheck official current sources before stating law, thresholds, platform rules, or deadlines. Store citations and observation dates in the bid workspace rather than copying broad legal conclusions into the reusable profile.
